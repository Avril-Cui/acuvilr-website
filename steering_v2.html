Context Pucksv2 · tuning with five knobs

The app already shows each idea as a ring of 3–5 concepts around its puck. On the v2 puck that ring becomes physical: each concept takes a knob, in ring order from the pointer. Distance from the puck means semantic distance, so pull a knob out to go deeper into its concept, push it in to drop it, and pull a spare knob to grow a new one, or flip a knob white side up to rule its concept out of a merge. The ring's marks thicken clockwise, so the side of A facing B shows how much of B a mix takes.
knob → one of the idea's concepts out → deeper (1–4) · in → drop knob flipped → ruled out of merges thick side toward B → more B
